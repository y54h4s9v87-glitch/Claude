1. Open https://business.facebook.com. Use the account switcher at the top left to select the business that owns **{handle}**. {login_hint}
2. Click **Create reel** (from Home or *Content*/*Planner*).
3. In **Share to**, tick the Instagram account **{handle}** only. {share_to_facebook}
4. **Add video** -> upload from desktop -> choose the job's file. Wait until it finishes uploading.
5. **Reel details / caption**: paste the job's CAPTION into the Instagram caption box (and nothing in other boxes).
6. Leave cover, audio and editing alone. Click **Next** until you reach **Scheduling options**.
7. Choose **Schedule**, set the job's date and time, and click **Schedule** (not *Share now*).
8. Open **Planner** (or *Content -> Scheduled*) and confirm the reel is there at the right time. Put the link if shown, otherwise `scheduled-in-business-suite` as the result URL.
